# External reporting/provenance representation baseline

**Evidence label:** `DERIVED_EVIDENCE`

Three instantiated reporting representations were subjected to the same nine deterministic audit questions: aggregate-only reporting, a flat provenance row, and the SCAA bivariate contract. The questions cover population count, sample/hash identity, raw observation, tool version, reason text, source hash, typed claim-to-observation traversal, not-supported versus unobserved distinction, and closure completeness.

| Representation | Answerable queries | Total |
|---|---:|---:|
| AGGREGATE_ONLY | 2 | 9 |
| FLAT_PROVENANCE | 6 | 9 |
| SCAA_BIVARIATE_CONTRACT | 9 | 9 |

This is a field-and-link presence comparison over three landed trace cases. It is not a participant study, usability measurement, accuracy comparison, or general superiority result. A controlled independent reviewer study remains required for human-utility claims.
