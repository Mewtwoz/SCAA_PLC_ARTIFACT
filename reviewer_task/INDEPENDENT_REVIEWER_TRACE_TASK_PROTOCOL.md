# Independent reviewer trace-task protocol

**Evidence label:** `TEMPLATE_ONLY`

## Purpose

This protocol is designed to test whether evidence cards improve claim tracing relative to aggregate-only and raw-log presentations. No participant results exist in this package.

## Design

Use a randomized, counterbalanced within-participant design with three presentation conditions: aggregate-only, raw-log, and evidence-card. Each participant answers matched tasks covering evidence-source location, unsupported-versus-negative distinction, hash/rule verification, uncertainty identification, and over-broad-claim rejection. Rotate task instances across conditions to avoid exposing the same sample twice. Record correctness, completion time, confidence, and error category. Obtain any ethics or institutional approval required before recruitment.

## Analysis plan

Report participant characteristics, exclusions, task-level counts, paired effect estimates with confidence intervals, ordering effects, and disagreements. Do not interpret this protocol as evidence of utility until independent participants complete it.
